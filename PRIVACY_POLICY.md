## 2. Information we collect

We may collect:

- Account and profile information, such as your name, email address, organization name, and login credentials.

- Communications you send to support.

## 3. How we use information

We use personal information to provide, secure, maintain, and improve the Service; create and administer accounts; process subscriptions; provide support; prevent fraud or misuse; comply with legal obligations; and communicate material service or policy changes.

We process information only for declared, legitimate purposes and collect information that is reasonably necessary for those purposes.

## 4. When we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the Service, including hosting, communications, security, and payment providers such as Stripe. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a lawful business transfer.

We do not sell personal information.

## 5. International processing

Our providers may process information outside the Philippines. When we use providers that process personal information internationally, we will take reasonable steps to use appropriate safeguards and comply with applicable data-protection requirements.

## 6. Retention and deletion

After cancellation, we retain account data for 90 days so that you can export it or reactivate your account. We may retain limited information for longer where required or permitted by law, to resolve disputes, maintain security, or meet payment-provider and tax obligations. When data is no longer required, we will delete or anonymize it using reasonable safeguards.

## 7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, object to or withdraw consent for certain processing, and request information about our processing. You may export your account data during the 90-day post-cancellation retention period. Contact us at jleward.escote17@gmail.com to make a request.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version and change its "Last updated" date. For material changes, we will provide additional notice when required by law.

## 10. Philippine privacy rights

If the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 applies to you, you may have rights to be informed about the collection and processing of your personal data and to exercise the rights described above. You may also raise a concern with the National Privacy Commission where appropriate.
